The problem

Current solutions fall short.

Forestry offsets are vulnerable to fire and fraud. Direct air capture costs hundreds of dollars per tonne and demands enormous energy inputs. Raceway systems are water-intensive and low-productivity.

Biological sequestration has always had the right idea. The execution has been the problem. We're fixing that.

The system

A continuous loop from
sunlight to stable carbon.

01
Cultivate

Microalgae grow in deep serpentine open-channel cascades, absorbing atmospheric CO₂ via photosynthesis. Gravity-fed, open top, no artificial light required.

02
Harvest

A portion of the culture is continuously harvested via centrifuge and filtration press. The remainder recirculates to maintain a productive, stable culture.

03
Convert

Wet biomass enters the HTC reactor at 200°C, 20–35 bar, for 4–5 hours. No pre-drying required. Output: hydrochar with 100–300 year carbon stability.

04
Recirculate

Process water and heat are recovered and fed back into the cascade. IoT sensors monitor air quality and system health continuously.
